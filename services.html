Our Signature Service

Solvia Signature Care™

Concierge personal care and companionship delivered in the comfort of home — unhurried, dignified, and built entirely around your loved one. Choose the length of care that fits your family.

1dedicated hour

The Essentials

For the client who deserves unhurried, dignified personal care.

Request a Consultation

The Essentials is our foundational concierge personal care experience — one dedicated hour of focused, unhurried attention. Your loved one is never rushed. Every visit includes a full personal hygiene experience: assisted bathing, shampoo and hair care styled to their preference, shave or facial grooming, moisturizing with their preferred products, oral hygiene, dressing assistance, a fresh linen change, and a clinical skin observation documented by your caregiver. Throughout every moment your caregiver is present — not just performing tasks but genuinely connecting with your loved one as a companion. This is the time for your family to breathe.

2dedicated hours

The Signature

For the client who deserves more time, more presence, and more of everything.

Request a Consultation

The Signature is our most popular tier — two dedicated hours of White Glove personal care and companionship. Everything in the Essentials, plus extended grooming attention, nail care for hands and feet, a foot soak for ambulatory clients, extended skin assessment, and meaningful companion time — conversation, music, reading, engagement in whatever brings your loved one joy. For bed-bound clients, the Signature tier allows for a thorough, unhurried total care experience with full attention to every detail of comfort and dignity. Your family gets two hours of real respite. Your loved one gets two hours of feeling like the most important person in the room — because they are.

4dedicated hours

The Premium

For the family that needs real respite and the client who deserves a full experience.

Request a Consultation

The Premium is four dedicated hours of luxury concierge personal care and companionship. Everything in the Signature tier, plus extended companion presence — a full morning or afternoon with your loved one. Light meal preparation. Engagement activities tailored to their preferences and cognitive level. For bed-bound clients, four hours allows for the most thorough, gentle, and dignified total care experience available in the home setting. For ambulatory clients, it is a half-day of expert personal care and genuine human connection. Your family gets four hours to run errands, attend appointments, sleep, or simply be — knowing your loved one is in the most capable and caring hands available. This is Solvia at its fullest expression.

Foot soak and nail care (hands and feet) are provided for non-diabetic clients only. For diabetic clients, we note the need and refer appropriately.

Every Tier Includes — No Exceptions

Every Solvia Signature Care visit, at every tier, includes the following without exception.

  • Assisted bathing tailored to mobility level
  • Shampoo, hair care, and styling to preference
  • Shave or facial grooming
  • Moisturizing and skin care with preferred products
  • Oral hygiene
  • Dressing assistance
  • Fresh linen change
  • Clinical skin observation, documented every visit
  • Genuine companionship — never rushed, never transactional
  • Visit documentation submitted within 30 minutes
  • Family follow-up call after the first three visits
How Investment Works

Personalized care, personalized investment

Solvia does not publish rates. Every client and every family situation is unique. We offer a complimentary in-home consultation where we learn about your loved one, assess their needs, and present a personalized Care Investment Summary. Your investment reflects the complexity of care, the frequency of visits, and the specific needs of your loved one. Contact us to schedule your complimentary consultation.

Specialized Support

Private Duty Home Care

One-on-one, in-home support that grows with your loved one's needs — spanning companion care through hospice-level comfort.

1

Companion Care

Friendly presence, conversation, light routine support, and safety supervision that keeps clients engaged and never alone.

2

Memory & Cognitive Care Specialist

Patient, structured support for clients living with dementia and cognitive change — calm redirection, familiar routines, dignity first.

3

Behavioral Health Support

Steady, compassionate care for clients with behavioral health needs — consistency, de-escalation, and respect at every visit.

4

Home Rehabilitation Support

Hands-on assistance for clients recovering at home after hospital or rehab — safe mobility, fall prevention, and daily-living support.

5

Hospice Support Care

Gentle comfort care for clients in the final season of life. We work alongside your hospice team, honoring the client and supporting the whole family with presence and grace.
