Signature Care

Caregiver

Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)

Deliver total-care support including bed baths, transfers, and high-acuity personal care. Current CNA certification required. You bring the skill; we bring the standard and the support.

What We Look For

Our standard for every team member

The Solvia Standard starts with the people we choose. Every caregiver meets these before their first visit.

  • A genuine heart for serving elderly adults, people with disabilities, and families in transition
  • Reliability you can set a clock by — punctuality is non-negotiable
  • A clear background check (Solvia covers the cost for retained caregivers)
  • Current credentials for your role — CNA certification required for CNA-level assignments
  • Willingness to document thoroughly and follow HIPAA and safety protocols
  • Reliable transportation and professional, respectful conduct in every home
