Our Story

Care with dignity at the center

Solvia Health Services was founded on a simple conviction: how we care for the vulnerable reflects what we believe. Too many families are told there is nowhere to turn — no one available after hours, no one who will show up on time, no one who treats their loved one like a whole person instead of a task.

We built Solvia to be the answer. A premium in-home care company where credentialed caregivers deliver warm, thorough, dignified support — and where faith informs every standard we set. From personal hygiene to specialized private duty care, our promise never changes: the same excellent, personal experience in every home we enter.
